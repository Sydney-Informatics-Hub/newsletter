Learn statistical workflows to enhance your research


As a University of Sydney researcher, you can access free statistical support from the SIH statistical consulting unit, no matter what your current level of statistical knowledge is. Access this support through our modular statistical training system or submit a request for a one-on-one consultancy.
Integral to both services are our statistical workflows that give practical step-by-step instructions applicable in any software to ensure efficiency and best practice use of analytical methods. Our workflows have improved researchers efficiency in analysis as well as in our consultancy sessions. Their adoption empowers researchers to formulate appropriate questions and solve some of their problems on their own in order to excel and publish in the highest impact journals. 

Past attendees of our workshops have found the workflows very helpful: 
Very clear explanation and structure. I learnt that I can simply follow the same workflow for different types of analysis and seek help at early stages of study design. I used to skip over plotting graphs but now I can see how useful that step is.
Research Essentials is a great resource. I wish I could have undertaken this course in the early stages of my PhD prior to commencing my data analysis. I have wasted a lot of time going backwards and forwards.  I will know for next time.
Access all our statistical workshop hand-outs including all current statistical workflows via our Statistical Resources webpage or sign up for upcoming trainings via the SIH training calendar.

SIH Scheme Outcomes and Opportunities

SIH HPC Allocation Scheme 2022 Round 2

The Sydney Informatics Hub (SIH) HPC Allocation Scheme 2022 provides University of Sydney researchers access to time on the NCI Gadi supercomputer, the largest National HPC facility in the Southern Hemisphere. Computational time (Service Units) available through this scheme will be allocated according to project merit and on a reasonable fair-share basis. This scheme is supported by the DVC (Research). The 2022 Round 2 scheme will provide access to the NCI Gadi HPC Facility for Quarter 3 (Jul – Sept) and Quarter 4 (Oct - Dec), 2022.   
Current NCI projects supported by previous SIH HPC Allocation Rounds, the Sydney LIEF, or the Grand Challenges Scheme will need to apply to this scheme for an allocation in Q3 and Q4, 2022. 
Applications for the SIH HPC Allocation Scheme, Round 2 are now open, and will close 5th June.

Hacky Hour: Next session Wednesday, 18th of May

Hacky Hour is a regular meetup where all researchers – students, staff and university affiliates – gather in a social environment to collaborate and get research support. Experts and mentors from Sydney Informatics Hub and across the University will be available to advise and answer questions on coding, data analytics or digital tools.
Hacky Hour is held on the third Wednesday of every month: our upcoming sessions are at 3-4pm on the 18th of May and the 15th of June. You can join virtually via Zoom.
