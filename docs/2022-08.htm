News and Announcements

We are coming to Camden! Statistics workshops and drop-in session

We are excited about returning to the Camden campus on Tuesday 30th August for the 2022 SIH data bonanza! We will deliver workshops on linear and logistic regression modelling and how they relate to ANOVA, ANCOVA and mixed models as well as how to build multivariable models with many predictors. You will also have the opportunity to see our friendly statisticians face-to-face at Camden to ask us any data or analysis related questions. See you in the conference room on level 3 of the Centre for Carbon Water and Food (also on Zoom).  For more information see the description of each workshop and sign up via Eventbrite.
  • Linear Models 1: Register: Aug 30th 9:30-11:00am
  • Linear Models 2: Register: Aug 30th 11:30am-1:00pm
  • Statistical Model Building: Register: Aug 30th 2:00-3:30pm
  • Drop-in session @Camden - Ask our friendly statisticians questions about your analysis! No registration required. 3:30-4:30pm

Bespoke training workshops: Building external collaborations with PESA geoscience workshops

SIH is delivering a custom designed “Introduction to Python for Geoscience” course in collaboration with the external professional organisation, PESA. The course has been tailored to the technical geo-spatial problems commonly faced within the exploration and geoscience community. Our upcoming course is currently open for registrations (until sold out).
The team at SIH is made up of software engineers, statisticians, and data scientists, who all come with a variety of domain specialties (biology, geology, linguistics, physics, astronomy, bioinformatics, psychology, ecology, computer science, and more!). Get in touch with us to tailor an informatics upskilling customized for your research group.

SIH Scheme Outcomes and Opportunities

NCI Adapter Scheme Open for Computing Time in Q4

Congratulations to all the Sydney researchers who were successfully awarded time on Gadi, the Southern Hemisphere’s largest Supercomputer, for Q3 Adapter Scheme Allocations. And now the NCI Adapter Scheme is again open for allocations in Q4 (Oct, Nov, Dec) 2022. Applications will close on 14 August 2022 and NCI are running an online information session for applicants on the 2nd of August (register). The adapter scheme is designed to support start-up projects and oddly shaped jobs for use on the Gadi HPC, or requiring Gdata Storage solutions, and Nirin Cloud compute. Get in touch with us to discuss your application or visit the website for more details.

Hacky Hour: Next session Wednesday, 17th of August

Hacky Hour is a regular meetup where all researchers – students, staff and university affiliates – gather in a social environment to collaborate and get research support. Experts and mentors from Sydney Informatics Hub and across the University will be available to advise and answer questions on coding, data analytics or digital tools.
Hacky Hour is held on the third Wednesday of every month: our upcoming sessions are at 3-4pm on the 17th of August and the 21st of September. You can join virtually via Zoom.

Need help getting started with whole genome mapping and variant calling?

SIH work closely with our partners at the Australian BioCommons to develop services and technologies that make it easier for Australian life scientists to do bioinformatics on the command line. We’ve recently been exploring genomic data processing, which is challenging work, no matter how experienced you are.  
On the 24th of August we’ll be sharing some essential steps and considerations for working with genomic data in a webinar titled Getting started with whole genome mapping and variant calling on the command line. Join SIH bioinformatician Georgina Samaha for some tips on how to design, find, and evaluate mapping and variant calling pipelines that are right for you and your research questions, and how to get access to the computing resources you need to process your samples. You can find registration details and more information here.
