January 2023 Training Update
Dear Researchers,
Welcome back! Join us again this year at one of our many training sessions, or at our monthly Hacky Hour. This month there are also opportunities to apply for high performance computing resources, including PhD scholarships. Read more below.
Tim on behalf of the SIH training team

Hacky hour on tomorrow @ 3pm!

Hacky hour is a regular meetup where all researchers – students, staff, and university affiliates – gather in a social environment to collaborate and receive research support. Experts and mentors from Sydney Informatics Hub and across the University will be available to advise and answer questions on coding, data analytics, or digital tools.
Come along and bring your stats, coding, and data science problems for us to solve! See you there. 

AgReFed Data Harvester in Python workshop 

2 February, online

Due to the highly-attended R cohort, we are running a Python stream for the AgReFed Data Harvester. Come learn about reproducible workflows for collection of Geo-Data. This will also be of interest for anyone wanting to get involved in an early Open Source Project! Come help design the future!

View our training calendar to register for the workshop on the 2nd of February.

Foundational statistical workshops - planning your research

8 February, in-person and on Zoom

To support you with planning your research studies for 2023 we're offering a full day of our 'foundation' workshops. They're running on Wednesday 8th February at Camperdown campus and via Zoom. Attend Research Essentials, Experimental Design and Power and Sample Size to plan your study and set yourself up for research success! View our training calendar to register.

High Performance Computing courses at the University of Sydney

22 and 23 February, online

Your first HPC courses for the year are running in February! Learn all about our in-house supercomputer “Artemis” and the multi-petabyte Research Data Storage options at Sydney. Stop clogging up your laptop and using external USB sticks!
Register at the direct links below:
Introduction to Artemis HPC: https://www.eventbrite.com.au/e/511214826307
Data transfer and Research Data Storage: https://www.eventbrite.com.au/e/511217875427


Students and Postdocs wanted for international HPC summer school
9-14 July, Atlanta, GA, USA – applications close 31 January

The expense-paid internship will teach major state-of-the-art aspects of HPC and Big Data Analytics for a variety of scientific disciplines, catalyze the formation of networks, provide advanced mentoring, facilitate international exchange and open further career options for a scholar who could benefit from including advanced computing tools and methods into their existing work. Applicants from any research discipline are welcome, provided their research activities include computational work. If you have used Artemis or are planning to use Artemis, this means you! Apply before the January 31 deadline.

Starting or currently doing a PhD? Need cash and computing resources? NCI HPC-AI Scholarships open now! 
Applications close 31 January

NCI is looking to support AI applications on diverse scientific problems such as solving differential equations, accelerating molecular dynamics, predicting 3-dimensional protein structures, controlling nuclear fusion, or weather forecasting with higher resolution and accuracy. The (10) PhD scholarships provide 100KSU and $10,000 cash this year though their new HPC-AI talent program.  Application deadline is 31st of January 2023. Details may be found here: https://opus.nci.org.au/x/hYDeCw 
Apply Now >

NCI Adapter Scheme for HPC resources
Applications close 12 February

This round of the “Adapter” Scheme provides up to 250KSU of computing resources for projects during Q2 2023. The quarterly scheme is a great opportunity to shift your workloads off Artemis and get access to Australia’s National Compute Infrastructure.  Postdoctoral researchers are now eligible to apply as Lead CIs without needing funding directly attributable to them. 
Apply Now >
