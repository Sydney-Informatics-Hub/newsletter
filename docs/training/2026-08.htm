GenAI Workshops 

September and October: Zoom

Learn to use AI effectively in your research — without the guesswork.
Sydney Informatics Hub is running its GenAI workshop series again in September and October. Attended by over 1,600 researchers last year, these hands-on online workshops give you practical strategies for using AI across every stage of your research — from the basics through to data analysis, coding, and beyond. 
21 September, 10am-12pm - GenAI 101: Fundamentals 
Understand how large language models actually work, what their limits are, and how to choose the right AI tool for your research context — including running one locally on your own laptop.
22 September, 10am-12pm - GenAI 201: Prompting 
Learn how context and prompt structure shape AI outputs, and which prompting strategies to use — and avoid — depending on your task.
23 September, 10am-12pm - GenAI 202: Reading & Writing 
Explore the tradeoffs of different AI strategies for finding papers, reading literature, and writing — so you can use them effectively without being misled by them.
12 October, 10am-12pm - GenAI 203: Tools 
Cut through the noise — learn a practical framework for matching AI tools to your research tasks, and leave knowing exactly where each tool helps and where it doesn’t.
14 October, 2pm-4pm - GenAI 301: Data Analysis 
Learn tool-agnostic strategies for using AI across your data analysis pipeline — and where to never let it take the wheel.
15 October, 10am-12pm - GenAI 303: Qualitative Analysis 
Explore how AI can support qualitative research workflows — and where human judgement remains essential.
15 October, 2pm-4pm - GenAI 302: Coding 
From understanding existing code to automating entire projects — learn how AI can help at every level of your coding workflow without losing control.
Keep an eye on our training calendar for updates.

SIH brings Data Expertise to Camden Campus – all welcome!  

25 August Hybrid workshops: in-person or Zoom

Sydney Informatics Hub's Statistical Consulting Unit (SCU) are excited to return to the Camden campus on Tuesday 25 August for an informal drop-in session and to deliver three training workshops.
The afternoon face-to-face only session from 3:30pm to 4:30pm will give attendees the opportunity to ask our friendly statisticians any questions regarding their research data, study design or statistical analysis – no registration required.
The three 90-minute, software agnostic workshops earlier in the day will focus on 'foundational' concepts and skills for planning any data analysis. Attendees will gain step-by-step statistical workflows and practical knowledge that is directly applicable to their research projects. Attend Research Essentials – Analysing your Data, Experimental Design and Power and Sample Size to plan your study and analysis and set yourself up for grant and research success!

For more information and registration, please visit the SIH training calendar.

Teach AI your codebook: Free Wordflow workshop

28 August: Zoom

Text analytics without code, including GenAI text coding - do you work with text (interviews, social media, news, archives, etc.), but not with Python or R? Join us on Friday 28 August for a free online workshop on LDaCA Wordflow, a point-and-click text analytics app from the Language Data Commons of Australia.
The morning session (11:00am - 12:30pm AEST) will be a watch-only tour of the concepts, the interface, and a worked-example research workflow will be built live. This session will be recorded for later.
The afternoon session (2:00pm - 3:30pm AEST) will be a hands-on session which will introduce Wordflow's new Annotation tool. 
Attend either session or both; no coding experience required. We hope to see you there!

Survey Design & Analysis day  

9 September Hybrid workshops: in-person or Zoom

Thanks to university-supported online platforms like Redcap and Qualtrics, delivering surveys to research participants is easier than ever. Asking the right questions within your surveys can be more of a challenge. Is it easy for your participants to understand what you are asking? Will analysis of the responses help you answer your research questions? Is your survey well designed? 
On September 9, Sydney Informatics Hub presents a day of workshops relevant to researchers who want to upskill in survey techniques. Each workshop is 90 minutes long and provides practical advice on methods used frequently in survey design and analysis. Surveys I covers design and analysis considerations common to most surveys. Surveys II delves into more advanced topics including assessing reliability and creating new indices using multiple questions.  

Register now for one or both surveys workshops to ensure that your next survey will yield valuable information for your research.

Hacky Hour is tomorrow! 

19 August, 2:00-3:00pm: Zoom

Hacky Hour is a regular meetup held the third Wednesday of every month where researchers – students, staff, and university affiliates – can gather in a social environment to collaborate and receive research support.
Hacky Hour is free, informal and everyone is welcome to drop in for advice, or offer their expertise to help others. 
SIH staff will be available to advise on topics such as high performance computing (HPC), general programming, R, Python, stats and data management. We hope to see you there!
