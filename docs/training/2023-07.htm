Hacky Hour on tomorrow @ 3pm!

19 July, online

Hacky Hour is a regular meetup where all researchers – students, staff, and university affiliates – can gather in a social environment to collaborate and receive research support. Experts and mentors from Sydney Informatics Hub and across the University will be available to advise and answer questions on coding, data analytics, or digital tools.
Come along and bring your stats, coding, and data science problems for us to solve! See you there.

SIH Masterclass: Data Storytelling

27 July: online

Join us for an engaging and informative training session on data storytelling, designed to equip you with the skills and knowledge to effectively convey the "story" of your research through data visualization. We will explore various data storytelling techniques and introduce you to the tools available to visualize your data effectively.
Please note: this session will be recorded and slides made available to all attendees. Register now!
When: 27 July, 3-4pm
Where: Zoom only

We are coming to Camden!

16 August, hybrid workshops and in-person drop-in session

We are excited about returning to the Camden campus on Wednesday 16th August for the 2023 SIH data bonanza! We will deliver our 'foundation' workshops for planning any data analysis. Attend “Research Essentials”, “Experimental Design” and "Power and sample size" to plan your study and set yourself up for research success! You will also have the opportunity to see our friendly statisticians face-to-face at Camden to ask us any data or analysis related questions. See you in the General Teaching Building (GTB), Room 106 A/B (also on Zoom for workshops).  For more information see the description of each workshop and sign up via Eventbrite.
• Research Essentials – analysing your data, August 16th, 9:30-11:00am
• Experimental Design, August 16th, 11:30am-1:00pm
• Power and Sample Size Calculation, August 16th, 2:00-3:30pm
• Drop-in session @Camden - Ask our friendly statisticians questions about your study design or analysis! No registration required, 3:30-4:30pm
