Demystifying statistical modelling 

17 March Hybrid workshops:
in-person or Zoom

Do you get confused about the differences between linear and logistic regression or ANOVA, ANCOVA, repeated measures and mixed models?
On March 17 we will deliver three workshops from our modular statistical training system to demystify these terms and show how all of these align in the one Linear Models framework. Linear Models Module 1 explains how the common analyses of regression, ANOVA, ANCOVA and repeated measures can all be done using the same Linear Models workflow. Module 2 then extends this to the Generalised Linear Model (GLM) framework for non-normal distributions such as Logistic and Count/Poisson regression. Aligning all these different methods into the same box with the same generic workflow makes it much easier to understand and remember how to apply them, while the practical examples provided give clear instructions for later use. The final module on the day, Statistical Model Building, explores the issues around more complex statistical modelling to build multivariable models with many predictors.

Hacky Hour is tomorrow! 

18 February, 2-3pm: Zoom

Hacky Hour is a regular meetup held the third Wednesday of every month where researchers – students, staff, and university affiliates – can gather in a social environment to collaborate and receive research support.
Hacky Hour is free, informal and everyone is welcome to drop in for advice, or offer their expertise to help others. SIH staff will be available to advise on topics such as high performance computing (HPC), general programming, R, Python, stats and data management. We hope to see you there!
