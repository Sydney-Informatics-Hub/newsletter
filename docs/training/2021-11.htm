Hacky Hour

November 17th 3-4 pm Zoom

Just a heads up - this Wednesday we're again running our monthly "Hacky Hour" via Zoom, where researchers including PhD students can drop in to get help.
So if:
  • There is a bug in your code that's been bugging you (and could do with another pair of eyes - or serve as a rubber duck).

Statistics workshops – last chance for 2021

November 22nd - Zoom

Researchers take note: this is the last day of statistics workshops in 2021.  Do you need to improve your skill set in any of these areas? Each session is self-contained and designed for maximum impact in a compact 90 minute format.
“Experimental Design” properly done will maximise the value of your collected data and the impact of your research. Register: Nov22 9:30 - 11:00 am
“Power and Sample Size Calculation” is likewise an important consideration when planning your study. Register Nov22 11:30 am - 1:00 pm
“Multivariate statistical analysis 1: Dimension Reduction” has many uses.  Do you know what they are?  Register Nov22 1:30 pm - 3:00pm
