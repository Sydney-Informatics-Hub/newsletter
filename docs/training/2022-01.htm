Welcome to 2022! Please join us for our upcoming training sessions. In line with University guidelines, our training will continue online next month, although we hope to welcome you back in person sometime in 2022.

Statistics workshops return

February 9th 9:30-11, 11:30-1, 2-3:30 pm

Zoom 3x1.5 hour workshops


We kick off the year with three workshop topics that are vital for researchers undertaking all forms of quantitative research.  Last year for example over 250 people attended “Research Essentials – Analysing your data” to boost their analysis capability.  You can upskill in these topics in just 90 minutes per session.
Research Essentials – Analysing your data Is a suitable entry point for all researchers to understand the basics of a statistical analysis workflow Register: 9-Feb 9:30-11:00am
Experimental Design properly done will maximise the value of your collected data and the impact of your research Register: 9-Feb 11:30am-1:00pm
Power and Sample Size Calculation helps to inform your design and analysis plan thus optimising your research for a successful outcome  Register: 9-Feb 2:00-3:30pm
