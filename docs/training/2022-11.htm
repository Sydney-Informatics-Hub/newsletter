Experimental design and statistics workshops

6 December, in-person and on Zoom

Planning an experiment?
A critical stage in successful high-impact research is experimental design, which is much more than just choosing the right controls. With a solid command of experimental design techniques, you can escape bias and tame variability in your study population. Getting familiar with randomisation, blocking, sampling, blinding and replication will help you make the most of the precious time and resources spent running your experiments.
In the Experimental Design workshop, we will introduce you all the must-know techniques, and in the Power and Sample Size Calculation workshop we will show you how to choose the right sample size for a well-powered experiment. Sign up now for these two workshops, presented by SIH on December 6, along with Multivariate Statistical Analysis 1: Dimension Reduction.

SIH High Performance Computing Allocation Scheme 2023:

Round 1 applications now open 

The Sydney Informatics Hub (SIH) HPC Allocation Scheme 2023 provides University of Sydney researchers access to time on the NCI Gadi supercomputer, the largest National HPC facility in the Southern Hemisphere. Compute resources available through this scheme will be allocated according to project merit and on a reasonable fair-share basis. If you are looking for more compute or GPU power than Artemis can provide, this is a fantastic opportunity to get on to GADI.   

Applications for Round 1 of the Scheme (for resources in Q1 and Q2, 2023) are open now, and close Sunday 4th December.
