Statistics workshops for researchers

November 9th 9:30-11, 11:30-1, 2-3:30 pm

Zoom, 3x1.5 hour workshops

Next month our focus is on supporting researchers with 3 short training workshops that may be of special interest to those working in Medicine and Health (FMH, take note!). Each session is designed for maximum impact in a compact 90 minute format. 
“Research Essentials – Analysing your data” is the go-to first workshop for researchers analysing quantitative data. Applicable across all faculties. Register

“Meta-Analysis – An Introduction” continues to be very popular, perhaps because it is one research field that has remained viable during the Covid pandemic.  Anyone undertaking a systematic review should consider whether a meta-analysis is needed and how to do it (before you collect the data). Again, of interest not just for FMH... Register

“Introduction to Survival Analysis” This specialised analysis technique has its origins in studying human survival and death.  Very useful for longitudinal studies with tracking and follow up to detect or observe an event, whether it is death or something else entirely (like customer churn, for example!). Register

ResBaz Sydney 2021

November 23-25th

Online, many workshops & streams



The 2021 Sydney Research Bazaar (ResBaz) will be held fully online on November 23rd-25th. This multi-day, cross-university event is all about sharing and learning all things digital. Part conference, part workshops - all showcasing digital skills and their applications to research.
The program features key stories (like keynotes, but with a narrative!), workshops, short talks, demonstrations and lots of opportunities to find out about next gen tech and tools for researchers. There are dedicated streams for Compute, Humanities and Social Science, and Survey / Data Collection, as well as two streams for introductory programming skills, which are open to all, regardless of skill level or discipline. Lots of breaks, lots of opportunities to find your people across institutions and disciplines, and plenty of neat tricks and hacks to level up on your nerd skills.
Pro tip: You need to register separately for the main ResBaz event and separately express your interest in any ResBaz workshop(s) you're interested in attending!
