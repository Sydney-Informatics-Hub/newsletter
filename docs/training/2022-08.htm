ATAP workshop: Finding quotes and speakers in text using the ATAP quotation tool

Zoom 8 September: 12 pm - 1 pm

The Australian Text Analytics Platform (ATAP) is a project that aims to provide researchers with the tools and training for analysing, processing, and exploring text. As part of this project, the SIH is showcasing the first session of a series of workshops/tools, Quotation Tool, which is developed by the Discourse Processing Lab at Simon Fraser University and adapted with permission. 

The Quotation tool uses Natural Language Processing (NLP) approaches to extract quotes from large (or small) text datasets, all from the comfort of a web browser. The tool can identify from the sentence "Apple said that Tim Cook will be in Australia next month." the phrase "Tim Cook will be in Australia next month" as the quote and "Apple" as the speaker. It also allows users to extract and visualise additional information right in the web browser, including identifying named entities affiliated with the speakers and in the quotes themselves, such as "Apple", "Tim Cook" and "Australia" in the above example. The tool supports saving the results locally for further analysis, enabling you to gain meaningful insights into your research questions. 

This 1-hour online workshop will be of interest to researchers and HDR students in Linguistics, Business, Law and Science and others who are interested in working with text datasets - no background knowledge of NLP or coding experience is required.

BioCommons webinar: Running bioinformatics workflows using Nextflow on Pawsey Nimbus cloud 

Zoom - 20 September  12:00 pm -1:00 pm 

Bioinformaticians across the world are using the Nextflow to build and manage workflows. Many of these workflows are shared for others to use and are supported by the community via nf-co.re. These workflows cover common analyses in bioinformatics such as RNA-Seq, mapping, variant calling, single cell transcriptomics and more and can be easily deployed by anyone, regardless of skill level. 
On September 20th, Dr. Nandan Deshpande from the Sydney Informatics Hub, University of Sydney, will discuss how one can deploy the freely available nf.co-re workflows on the Pawsey Nimbus Cloud. In the latter half of the webinar, Dr. Sarah Beecroft from the Pawsey Supercomputing Research Centre will talk about their Pawsey Nimbus Cloud systems and why Nimbus is turning out to be one of the most favoured choices for researchers to deploy the nextflow workflows for their analysis.

BioCommons workshop: RNA-Seq analysis using Nextflow workflows 

Zoom - 27 & 28 September 2022, 2-5pm

RNA sequencing (RNA-Seq) is a common method used to understand the differences in gene expression and molecular pathways between two or more groups. This workshop introduces the fundamental concepts of RNA sequencing experiments and it will allow you to try out the analysis using data from a study of Williams-Beuren Syndrome, a rare disease. 
In the first part of the workshop, you will learn how to convert sequence reads into analysis ready count data. To do this we will use nf-core/rnaseq - a portable, scalable, reproducible, and publicly available workflow on the Pawsey Nimbus Cloud.  
In the second part of the workshop, you will use the count data which you have created to identify differential genes and pathways using R/RStudio. By the end of the workshop, you should be able to perform your own RNA-Seq analysis for differential gene expression and pathway analysis.
