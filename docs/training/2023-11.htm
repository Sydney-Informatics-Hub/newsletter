SIH High Performance Computing Scheme for hours in Q1/Q2 2024 

Missed NCMAS? Artemis not up to the job? Apply for the Sydney Scheme! 
The team at the Sydney Informatics Hub help to bridge the gap for University of Sydney researchers whose high performance computing needs are not met by Artemis or other infrastructure. We provide access to national computational resources at NCI via the SIH HPC Scheme. This merit-based scheme has a manageable application process that researchers can use after they outgrow Artemis or if they miss out on NCMAS.
Round 1 of the SIH HPC scheme, which determines quarterly allocations for the first half of 2024, is now open. Please apply here.
