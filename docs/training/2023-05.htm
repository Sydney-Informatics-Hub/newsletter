SIH Masterclass: Distributional Regression modeling: Raise your research to the next level 

25 May: in-person or Zoom

Elevate your research and come to the SIH masterclass on distributional regression! The classical linear model and its generalized extension (GLM) are widely used in applied statistics. However, required model assumptions are frequently not met, resulting in estimates of effects that can be biased. This masterclass will outline how distributional regression, an increasingly popular tool in regression analysis, overcomes these limitations by providing the ability to link distributional parameters beyond the mean to additive predictors, for a practically unlimited choice of response distributions. Meta-Analysis workshop

May 24: in-person or zoom

Are you currently working on a systematic review or plan to do so in the future? If yes, then it is important for you to know about meta-analysis, a statistical technique that can add a valuable quantitative component to your qualitative literature review. In contrast to primary studies that combine information across individual subjects, meta-analysis combines information across multiple studies. This powerful technique can provide a more precise estimate of the quantitative effect of a particular phenomenon, whether it be an educational intervention, drug treatment, disease incidence in the human population, or anything else.
In addition to increased accuracy, meta-analysis provides a quantitative snapshot of the existing research in your field, enabling you to compare the contribution of individual studies to an overall estimate. You can use the technique to better understand conflicting results across multiple studies by examining potential publication bias, and the effect of different population or study subgroups. To learn more about how meta-analysis works, and how you can incorporate it into your research projects, sign up to the free Sydney Informatics Hub meta-analysis workshop happening on May 24!

Exploring Digital Text Collections with Juxtorpus: A Taster Webinar on the Latest ATAP Text Analysis Tool 

May 25: in-person or Zoom

Join us for a hybrid webinar on the latest addition to the suite of ATAP text analysis tools - Juxtorpus. Developed to provide a unified framework for managing and exploring text contents and metadata, Juxtorpus offers a Corpus package that enables flexible building, exploration, and slicing of your corpus whilst maintaining its shape. It also offers a Jux package that allows for easy comparison and highlighting of differences between any two corpora with tools and visualisations that come off-the-shelf. During the workshop, we'll also show you how to use other ATAP tools in combination with the Corpus to create a reusable workflow that will boost your analysis capabilities. 
We invite anyone interested in learning how to handle and analyse their digital text collections to join us. No programming knowledge or skills are required, and there will be minimal hands-on opportunities during this session. Register to reserve your spot today. 
When: 25 May, 10:30am – 12pm
