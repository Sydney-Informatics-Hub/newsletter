Dear Researchers,
In line with the public health order, all SIH training will be delivered online via Zoom for the foreseeable future, with full-day training split across two ½ days. In this update we'd like to highlight some of our flagship training for which we have registrations open. Find the full list of our training events below or at our training calendar.

Machine Learning in R:

Regression (MLR1)

July 22nd & 23rd, 9:00 am - 12:30 pm

Zoom, full day split into 2 x ½ days

In this workshop you'll learn about exploring and wrangling data to make it suitable for running a machine learning analysis, and some classic and cutting-edge methods for predicting a continuous quantitative outcome based on a mix of quantitative and qualitative features (aka regression). The training is suitable for researchers comfortable with R and the tidyverse; it doesn't assume any mathematical statistical knowledge. We recommend signing up to MLR2 (below) in conjunction with this course.
Register >

Machine Learning in R: Classification & Unsupervised Learning (MLR2)

July 29th & 30th, 9:00 am - 12:30 pm

Zoom, full day split into 2 x ½ days

In this training, you'll learn about what's different when you have a categorial outcome instead of a continuous one (classification). We'll also show how to just "look" at your data to try to identify some patterns in it, when you don't have a clear outcome to model (unsupervised learning).
Similar to MLR1, this training is suitable for researchers comfortable using R and the tidyverse; it does not assume any mathematical or statistical knowledge. We recommend attending MLR1 prior to attending this course. We maintain separate registration so people who can only attend one training event are able to sign up to the session they can make, and then sign up for the next iteration of MLR2/MLR1
