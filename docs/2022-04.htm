Asian Business in Australia

Congratulations to Dr Wei Li and Professor Hans Hendrischke from the Business School on the launch of the Asian Business in Australia research report as the outcome of an Industry Partnership Grant with the Commonwealth Bank Australia. This report has attracted multiple media attention from The Australian and the Gold Coast Bulletin, an interview on SBS and Chinese state media, the Xinhua News Agency. The Sydney Informatics Hub has proudly supported the data analysis work in this research and developed an interactive dashboard for presenting how Australian industries impacted the global economy through Asian countries between 1995 and 2018. The dashboard will become publicly available online soon, and more details about this report can be found here.

2022 Australasian Leadership Computing Grants at NCI now open 

Do you have an ambitious research project that pushes the boundaries of computational science? The 2022 round of Australasian Leadership Computing Grants (ALCG) is now open! NCI is offering 20-50 MSU allocations per project on the Gadi supercomputer. All fields of research are encouraged to apply, however successful applicants will need to demonstrate previous experience with high performance computing and a record of research outputs in computational sciences. In previous years, ALCG grants have made research projects involving global climate modelling, quantum chemical calculations, supermassive black hole simulations, and large-scale viral modelling possible.  
To kick off your application you’ll need to first lodge an Expression of Interest (EOI) to NCI by the 10th of April 2022. Successful EOIs will then be invited to submit an application, due 8th May 2022. For more information, see NCI’s website. If you’d like some advice or assistance in putting together your EOI, please contact the Sydney Informatics Hub at sih.info@sydney.edu.au.

Data Scientist (Text Analytics)

SIH is currently hiring a new Data Scientist (Text Analytics) to join our Informatics Team. This one-year fixed term role will develop compelling, accessible and reusable demonstration notebooks and data-driven research case studies as part of the Australian Research Data Commons' Australian Text Analytics Platform (ATAP) project, facilitating self-service researcher training and research acceleration. The internal application closes on Wednesday, 6th of April. If you are not currently a University of Sydney employee or need more information about this role, please don’t hesitate to contact Chao Sun, the hiring manager, with any inquiries.

SIH Scheme Outcomes and Opportunities

SIH HPC Allocation Scheme 2022 Round 2

The Sydney Informatics Hub (SIH) HPC Allocation Scheme 2022 provides University of Sydney researchers access to time on the NCI Gadi supercomputer, the largest National HPC facility in the Southern Hemisphere. Computational time (Service Units) available through this scheme will be allocated according to project merit and on a reasonable fair-share basis. This scheme is supported by the DVC (Research). The 2022 Round 2 scheme will provide access to the NCI Gadi HPC Facility for Quarter 3 (Jul – Sept) and Quarter 4 (Oct - Dec), 2022.   
Current NCI projects supported by previous SIH HPC Allocation Rounds, the Sydney LIEF, or the Grand Challenges Scheme will need to apply to this scheme for an allocation in Q3 and Q4, 2022. 
Applications for the SIH HPC Allocation Scheme, Round 2 will open 2nd May and close 5th June. You can preview the application form at the link below.

Hacky Hour: Next session Wednesday, 20th of April

Hacky Hour is a regular meetup where all researchers – students, staff and university affiliates – gather in a social environment to collaborate and get research support. Experts and mentors from Sydney Informatics Hub and across the University will be available to advise and answer questions on coding, data analytics or digital tools.
Hacky Hour is held on the third Wednesday of every month: our upcoming sessions are at 3-4pm on the 20th of April and the 18th of May. You can join virtually via Zoom.
