Fostering global connections: Japanese delegates explore research support offerings at the Sydney Informatics Hub  

On Thursday, September 12, we had the pleasure of welcoming five international delegates from Japan to the University of Sydney! The visit was a collaborative effort with the Publishing and Data Services team at Fisher library, the Research Data Strategy team, and the Sydney Informatics Hub. Our guests included Professor Kazuhiro Hayashi, Dr Chihiro Kunimoto, and three young librarians representing the Japanese National Institute of Science and Technology Policy within Japan’s Ministry of Culture, Sports, Science, and Technology. Their visit aimed to foster connections and spark conversations on research data sharing and open science.
After a delightful lunch at Fisher Library, we headed to the Sydney Informatics Hub offices at Moore College. Here, we introduced the delegates to our services, which include project support and one-on-one consulting in data science, bioinformatics, research computing and statistics. They were particularly interested in our Statistical Resources page and our range of statistical workshops which offer best-practice workflows for research topics such as effective study design. The delegates shared that, upon returning to Japan, they planned to write introductory articles for Japanese librarians to assist researchers in data management and best-practice research. 
Thank you to our Japanese delegates for visiting SIH! We hope you found our services inspiring and we look forward to future collaborations. 

SIH Statistical Consulting Unit at Westmead  

Our SIH Statistical Consulting Unit (SCU) visited the Westmead campus last month to deliver our most popular statistical workshop “Research Essentials – Analysing your Data”. We received over 100 registrations for the 90-minute, software-agnostic workshop that provides a systematic workflow suitable for any quantitative analysis and helps researchers plan their data analysis. 
Following the workshop, our statistical consultants and researchers from the fields of medicine, nursing and midwifery, allied health, oral health, population health, integrated/community care and mental health appreciated the opportunity to discuss statistical issues in the drop-in session.
The SCU team also had the opportunity to meet with Western Sydney Local Health District statisticians and Research and Education Network staff to discuss continued engagement and Community of Practice. We are currently exploring further in-person training and statistical consulting opportunities at Westmead for 2025. 
Learn more about the SCU team and the services they provide on our Statistical Resources page. 

SIH Staff Profile: Omar Arnaiz, Statistical Consultant

Omar is a Statistical Consultant at the Sydney Informatics Hub. He has been a statistician since 2012, working with a wide range of researchers from government and non-government organisations to private research companies in a range of domains such as ecology and environmental monitoring, public health, political, social, and market research. 
Since starting at SIH, Omar has particularly enjoyed the diversity of consults and projects. Some recent projects include modelling the association between socio-economic status and covid severity as well as creating a dementia detection model to be used in an online clinical tool. He is also looking forward to delivering our Surveys 1 and 2 workshops in the new year. 
Outside of the office, Omar can be found in the MMA gym or out exploring national parks on his motorbike (pictured, a bit lost but happy after finding unexpected cows hiding down the trail).

SIH Masterclass: No-Code Data Science using Orange

28 November, 3:00-4:00pm: Zoom

Have you ever wanted to play around with some machine learning or data wrangling tools beyond Excel, and yet been hesitant to go down the rabbit hole of installing R or Python (or Julia or Matlab or something else... who knows?!?!?...). Then our upcoming Masterclass on No-Code Data Science using Orange is just for you! We’ll showcase and share workflows for powerful machine learning models and data munging without coding using Orange, an open-source software with an intuitive graphical interface. You’ll learn how to perform exploratory data analysis, visualize complex data, and build and evaluate predictive models without writing a single line of code. Whether you're new to the field or looking to expand your skills, we will provide you with a step-by-step introduction to the world of machine learning and the benefits of using Orange
Key Highlights (or why we sometimes use Orange at SIH too!): 
• Rapid Prototyping: Quickly develop and test models to gain insights faster. 
• Connected Workflows: Build sophisticated layers of data transformations that are designed to work together. 
• Dynamic Visuals: Create interactive visual representations of your data and model results, facilitating understanding and communicating results.
Make complex analyses accessible with another tool in your toolbox! Register today!
