News and Announcements

Introducing our new Informatics Team Lead: Gordon McDonald


We are pleased to announce the appointment of Dr Gordon McDonald as the Informatics Team Lead at SIH. Gordon will manage the Informatics program and work across the University to realise SIH’s mission of supporting data driven research excellence at the University over a broad range of disciplines. Gordon has been with SIH almost since its beginning, and has worked on and managed a wide range of projects across data science, software engineering, and statistics. Gordon’s background is in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics (PhB, Australian National University, Uni Medal 2009), and he completed his PhD in experimental quantum mechanics (2015) at the Australian National University. Apart from his depth and breadth of technical experience, Gordon has also trained, supported, and mentored a number of SIH staff and we are very thankful for his leadership and guidance!  

If you would like to work with SIH on your data driven research project, contact us at sih.info@sydney.edu.au 

Scheme outcomes and opportunities

SIH High Performance Computing Allocation Scheme 2023: Round 1 applications closing soon

Applications close December 4th: Apply


The Sydney Informatics Hub (SIH) HPC Allocation Scheme 2023 provides University of Sydney researchers access to time on the NCI Gadi supercomputer. Compute resources available through this scheme will be allocated according to project merit and on a reasonable fair-share basis. If you are looking for more compute or GPU power than Artemis can provide, this is a fantastic opportunity to get on to GADI.     
 
Applications for Round 1 of the Scheme (for resources in Q1 and Q2, 2023) are open now, and close Sunday 4th December.

Training and Events

Foundational statistical workshops: February 8th

It’s almost University shutdown season, but it's also grant season! To support you with planning your research studies for 2023 we're offering a full day of our 'foundation' workshops. They're running on Wednesday 8th February at Camperdown campus and via Zoom. Attend “Research Essentials”, “Experimental Design” and "Power and sample size" to plan your study and set yourself up for research success! View our training calendar to register.

Hacky Hour resumes 18th of January, 2023

Thank you to the 100+ researchers who have joined us in our Hacky Hour sessions over 2022 – we have enjoyed meeting you, hearing about your research, tackling code and data management challenges and discussing all things tech with you. Our most popular topics for the year have been in statistics, R, SPSS, Python, research data management and bioinformatics! 

Thank you to Dr Henry Lydecker for hosting our hacky hour sessions, and to all our regular hackers. We've received lots of great feedback about these sessions:
Just wanted to say thank you so much for running hacky hour and help with my issue... I have found the hacky hour sessions incredibly helpful with navigating throughout this year..!
If you have attended Hacky Hour or are interested in attending next year, please check out our website and provide us with feedback, ideas for new topics, or suggestions. Our Zoom sessions have been very successful but please let us know if you're interested in in-person hacking!
Hacky Hour is held on the third Wednesday of every month: there's no session in December, but we'll return on the 18th of January, 2023. You can join virtually via Zoom.
